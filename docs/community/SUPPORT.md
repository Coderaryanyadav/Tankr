# Getting Support for TANKR

We are committed to providing a reliable experience for developers, water logistics operators, and municipal partners.

---

## 1. Documentation & Self-Service Guides

Before opening an issue or contacting support, check our technical documentation:
- [Installation Guide](docs/setup/INSTALLATION.md)
- [Environment Configuration](docs/setup/ENVIRONMENT_SETUP.md)
- [Troubleshooting Common Issues](docs/setup/TROUBLESHOOTING.md)
- [Architecture Overview](docs/architecture/ARCHITECTURE.md)

---

## 2. GitHub Issue Tracker

For bug reports and technical improvements:
- Search existing [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Coderaryanyadav/Tankr/issues) to ensure your topic has not already been addressed.
- Use our [Bug Report Template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug_report.yml) or [Feature Request Template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/feature_request.yml).
- Provide logs, environment details, and clear steps to reproduce.

---

## 3. Direct Contact Channels

- **Technical Inquiries & Security**: `security@tankr.in`
- **Municipal Logistics & Fleet Onboarding**: `partnerships@tankr.in`
- **General Inquiries**: `support@tankr.in`

---

## 4. Response Times

- **Critical Security Defects**: Acknowledged within 24 hours.
- **GitHub Issues**: Reviewed by maintainers within 2-3 business days.
